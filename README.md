## Volcanoes & Major Summits

### The 6,000m+ Giants (Seismiles)

- **[Aconcagua (6,961m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/1/Aconcagua)** - The highest peak in the Western and Southern Hemispheres, and the tallest mountain on Earth outside Asia. Located in Mendoza, Argentina.
- **[Cerro Bonete Chico (6,759m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/407/Bonete_Chico)** - One of the highest volcanic domes in the world, towering in the isolated La Rioja Puna in Argentina.
- **[Cerro Marmolejo (6,108m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/2/Marmolejo)** - The southernmost 6,000-meter peak on the planet, rising along the Chile-Argentina border in the Central Andes.
- **[Cerro Mercedario (6,720m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/16/Mercedario)** - The crowning summit of the Cordillera de la Ramada in San Juan, Argentina; famed for the historic Polish Glacier route.
- **[Cerro Pissis (6,795m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/26/Pissis)** - An enormous multi-peaked extinct volcanic complex deep within the remote Argentine Puna, second in volcanic height only to Ojos del Salado.
- **[Cerro Pular (6,233m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/cerro-pular-ascent)** - A wind-scoured high ridge summit in the Antofagasta Altiplano, requiring remote off-road navigation.
- **[Huascarán Sur (6,768m)](https://www.summitpost.org/huascaran/150244)** - The highest summit in Peru and the entire Earth's tropical zone, dominating the glaciated Cordillera Blanca.
- **[Nevado Sajama (6,542m)](https://www.summitpost.org/nevado-sajama/150410)** - Bolivia's highest mountain, an extinct stratovolcano rising dramatically above the Altiplano surrounded by dwarf *Polylepis tarapacana* forests.
- **[Nevado Tres Cruces (6,749m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/52/Tres_Cruces_Sur)** - A massive volcanic massif featuring three distinct 6,000m summits (Sur, Central, and Norte) within the Atacama region.
- **[Ojos del Salado (6,893m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/ojos-del-salado-volcano-ascent)** - The highest active volcano on Earth and second-highest summit in the Americas, situated in the high-desert Puna de Atacama.
- **[Volcán Aucanquilcha (6,176m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/aucanquilcha-volcano-ascent)** - Notable for housing the highest permanent human settlement in history (a sulfur mining camp at 5,950m) and offering sweeping views of the Bolivian salt flats.
- **[Volcán Chimborazo (6,263m)](https://www.summitpost.org/chimborazo/150349)** - An ice-capped stratovolcano in Ecuador whose summit is the closest point on Earth's surface to the sun due to equatorial bulge.
- **[Volcán Llullaillaco (6,739m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/llullaillaco-volcano-ascent)** - The world's highest archaeological sanctuary, where Incan ceremonial platforms and the preserved "Children of Llullaillaco" mummies were discovered.
- **[Volcán Pili / Acamarachi (6,046m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/pili-volcano-ascent)** - A steep, symmetrical volcanic cone with an Incan summit sanctuary overlooking the Aguas Calientes salt flat.
- **[Volcán San Pedro (6,145m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/san-pedro-volcano-ascent)** - An active twin-crater stratovolcano looming over the Upper Loa basin and the Atacama oasis.
- **[Volcán Walter Penck / Cazadero (6,658m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/walter-penck-volcano-ascent)** - A colossal volcanic massif in the Argentine Puna named in honor of the pioneering German geomorphologist.

### Active & Fumarolic Stratovolcanoes

- **[Volcán Cotopaxi (5,897m)](https://www.summitpost.org/cotopaxi/150311)** - One of the highest historically active volcanoes on Earth, providing a classic glaciated volcanic ascent in Ecuador.
- **[Volcán Láscar (5,592m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/lascar-volcano-ascent)** - The most active volcano in northern Chile, featuring a deep multi-crater caldera, fumarolic vents, and permanent SERNAGEOMIN seismic surveillance.
- **[Volcán Licancabur (5,920m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/licancabur-volcano-ascent)** - The postcard-perfect volcanic cone towering over San Pedro de Atacama and Laguna Verde, harboring one of Earth's highest crater lakes.
- **[Volcán Ollagüe (5,868m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/ollague-volcano-ascent)** - An active stratovolcano on the Chile-Bolivia frontier featuring an active sulfur fumarole clearly visible from its flanks.
- **[Volcán Parinacota (6,348m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/43/Parinacota)** - Symmetrical twin volcano in Lauca National Park rising above Lake Chungará with an 800m-wide crater rim.
- **[Volcán Putana (5,890m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/putana-volcano-ascent)** - A prominent stratovolcano with continuous solfataric venting emitting sulfur plumes across its crater rim.

### 5,000m+ Acclimatization Summits

- **[Cerro Corona (5,291m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/cerro-corona-ascent)** - A panoramic volcanic peak near the Argentine border providing sweeping vistas across the Salar de Atacama basin.
- **[Cerro El Plomo (5,424m)](https://www.andeshandbook.org/montanismo/cerro/25/El_Plomo)** - The symbolic Inca sanctuary mountain of the Central Andes above Santiago, essential for high-altitude preparation in Chile.
- **[Cerro Jorquencal (4,990m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/cerro-jorquencal-ascent)** - A scenic pre-Cordilleran ridge hike offering an ideal first half-day high-altitude acclimatization step.
- **[Cerro Sairecabur (5,971m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/sairecabur-volcano-ascent)** - A volcanic caldera group on the Chile-Bolivia border, accessible via old sulfur mining tracks with scree slopes and high-altitude exposure.
- **[Cerro Toco (5,604m)](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/expeditions/cerro-toco-ascent)** - Extinct stratovolcano located inside the ALMA astronomy reserve. The premier first 5,000m+ climb in Northern Chile due to high vehicle access (~5,000m).
- **[Volcán Chachani (6,057m)](https://www.summitpost.org/chachani/150243)** - Non-technical volcanic giant near Arequipa, Peru, widely used as an acclimatization ascent prior to higher peaks.

### Iconic Alpine Peaks & Patagonian Granites

- **[Cerro Torre (3,128m)](https://www.pataclimb.com/climbingareas/chalten/torregroup/torre.html)** - Legendary sheer granite spire crowned with precarious rime ice mushrooms, regarded as one of the supreme challenges in world alpinism.
- **[Huayna Potosí (6,088m)](https://www.summitpost.org/huayna-potosi/150295)** - Classic alpine glacier climb in Bolivia's Cordillera Real, featuring crevassed terrain, bergschrund crossings, and a narrow summit ridge.
- **[Monte Fitz Roy / Cerro Chaltén (3,405m)](https://www.pataclimb.com/climbingareas/chalten/fitzgroup/fitz.html)** - World-renowned technical granite monolith in Patagonia notorious for extreme storms and demanding technical mixed routes.
- **[Nevado Alpamayo (5,947m)](https://www.summitpost.org/alpamayo/150314)** - Iconic fluted ice pyramid in the Cordillera Blanca, celebrated worldwide for its aesthetic symmetry (Ferrari and French Direct routes).
- **[Torres del Paine (Central 2,460m)](https://www.summitpost.org/torres-del-paine/150537)** - Monumental granite towers rising above the Patagonian steppe within Chilean Patagonia's flagship national park.

## Altitude Medicine & High-Altitude Physiology

- **[CDC Yellow Book: High-Altitude Travel & Altitude Illness](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/yellowbook/2024/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness)** - Authoritative public health reference for medical practitioners and expedition planners on acclimatization profiles, risk factors, and altitude pathophysiology.
- **[Effective Oxygen & Atmospheric Pressure in Altitude](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/altitude-to-oxygen)** - Barometric pressure calculation tool modeling atmospheric pressure decay and effective inspired oxygen fraction ($P_{i}O_2$) up to 7,000m.
- **[Instituto de Patología de la Altura (IPPA La Paz)](http://www.altitudeclinic.com/)** - Pioneering research clinic in Bolivia founded by Dr. Gustavo Zubieta, dedicated to high-altitude adaptation and chronic mountain sickness (Monge's disease) studies.
- **[International Society for Mountain Medicine (ISMM)](https://ismm.org/)** - Global scientific society dedicated to biomedical research on hypobaric hypoxia, alpine health, and wilderness physiology.
- **[Lake Louise Acute Mountain Sickness Score (AMS)](https://www.high-altitude-medicine.com/)** - The official consensus scoring criteria (revised 2018) to evaluate, diagnose, and score Acute Mountain Sickness, HAPE, and HACE.
- **[UIAA Medical Commission Guidelines](https://www.theuiaa.org/mountain-medicine/medical-advice/)** - International Mountaineering and Climbing Federation consensus recommendations on high-altitude travel, Diamox (Acetazolamide) prophylaxis, hydration, and safe ascent velocity.
- **[Wilderness Medical Society (WMS) Clinical Practice Guidelines](https://wms.org/)** - Evidence-based clinical guidelines for the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of acute altitude illnesses, including pharmacological dosage recommendations.

## Expedition Planning & High-Altitude Calculators

- **[4x4 Off-Road Fuel Autonomy for Remote Puna](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/offroad-4x4-fuel-autonomy)** - Essential range calculation accounting for high-altitude engine combustion loss (-10% power per 1,000m), sand friction, and 4WD low-range fuel consumption.
- **[Altitude Acclimatization Rate Planner](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/altitude-acclimatization-rate)** - Evaluates daily sleeping elevation gains against WMS safety guidelines (<500m/day above 3,000m) to minimize acute mountain sickness risk.
- **[GPX / KML Track Converter](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/gpx-kml-kmz-converter)** - Fast web tool for converting coordinates between GPX, KML, and KMZ formats for Garmin, Suunto, and phone GPS units.
- **[GPX Route Merger](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/gpx-merge-tracks)** - Utility to combine multiple GPS track segments and waypoint files into single contiguous expedition routes.
- **[High-Altitude Supplemental Oxygen Duration](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/high-altitude-oxygen-duration)** - Cylinder pressure and flow rate calculator (liters per minute) for high-altitude rescue and mountaineering contingency planning.
- **[Naismith & Tobler Hiking Time Calculator](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/hiking-time-naismith)** - Dynamic calculation tool integrating Naismith's rule, Tobler's hiking slope function, and high-altitude physiological delay multipliers.
- **[Respiratory Water Loss & Hydration Planner](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/respiratory-water-loss-altitude)** - Quantifies hyperventilation-driven fluid loss in hyper-arid alpine conditions like the Atacama Puna (<10% relative humidity).
- **[Snow Melting Fuel & Water Calculator](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/snow-melting-water-fuel-altitude)** - Calculates white gas or isobutane canister fuel mass required to melt snow and boil drinking water at reduced ambient pressures.
- **[Summit Weather Window & Thermal Index](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/summit-window-index)** - Evaluates wind speed, gust frequency, and temperature to determine viable summit assault windows.
- **[Water Boiling Point at Altitude](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/water-boiling-point-altitude)** - Calculates the exact boiling temperature drop at high elevations (~76°C at 6,800m), crucial for food rehydration and pathogen sterilization.
- **[Wind Chill & Thermal Sensation Calculator](https://vulcanoexpediciones.com/en/outdoor-calculators/wind-chill)** - Joint Action Group / Temperature Index (JAG/TI) windchill index calculator to anticipate frostbite exposure times in sub-zero summit ridge conditions.

## Weather Forecasting, Wind Models & Snowpack

- **[IANIGLA-CONICET Glacier & Snow Observatory](https://www.mendoza.conicet.gov.ar/ianigla/)** - Argentine institute for snow, glaciology, and environmental sciences monitoring Andean snowpack cover, water reserves, and glacier retreat.
- **[Meteo Exploration](https://www.meteoexploration.com/)** - Specialized high-mountain meteorological forecasts and snow models calibrated specifically for the Andes and Himalayas.
- **[Meteoblue](https://www.meteoblue.com/)** - Multi-model comparison (NEMS, ECMWF, NOAA) providing detailed atmospheric soundings, meteograms, and cloud cover layers.
- **[Mountain-Forecast.com](https://www.mountain-forecast.com/)** - Multi-elevation weather forecasts (base, mid, and summit stations) with freezing level trends for major Andean summits.
- **[Observatorio Argentino de Vigilancia Volcánica (OAVV - SEGEMAR)](https://oavv.segemar.gob.ar/)** - Argentine volcanic monitoring agency providing hazard maps, seismicity reports, and status bulletins for the Argentine Andes.
- **[SERNAGEOMIN Volcanic Alerts (Chile)](https://rnvv.sernageomin.cl/)** - Real-time volcanic activity monitor, seismicity reports, and exclusion zone bulletins for active peaks in Chile.
- **[Snow-Forecast.com](https://www.snow-forecast.com/)** - Freezing levels, snowfall accumulation maps, and freezing-altitude tracking for South American ranges.
- **[Ventusky](https://www.ventusky.com/)** - Fluid dynamic meteorological visualizer with live freezing-level isotherm, precipitation type, and gust overlays.
- **[Windy.com (ECMWF & GFS Models)](https://www.windy.com/)** - Essential high-altitude wind speed and freezing level (`0°C isotherm`) radar, crucial for Atacama summits where windchill regularly drops below -25°C.

## Satellite Imagery & Earth Observation

- **[Copernicus Browser / Sentinel Hub](https://browser.dataspace.copernicus.eu/)** - Free near real-time 10-meter multispectral satellite imagery (Sentinel-2) to inspect snowline levels, penitente formation, glacial crevasses, and road washouts before departing.
- **[Copernicus Global Land Service - Snow Cover](https://land.copernicus.eu/)** - Near-real-time satellite-derived snow extent and snow water equivalent data across mountain ranges.
- **[NASA Worldview / Earthdata](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/)** - Daily global MODIS and VIIRS satellite imagery for tracking regional dust storms, volcanic eruption plumes, and broad snow cover changes.
- **[USGS EarthExplorer / SRTM Elevation](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/)** - NASA Shuttle Radar Topography Mission 30m digital elevation models (DEM) and Landsat imagery for custom 3D terrain visualization.

## Cartography, Topo Maps & GPX Route Repositories

- **[Andeshandbook](https://www.andeshandbook.org/)** - Non-profit mountain directory documenting primary routes, historical first ascents, approach roads, and logistical details for peaks across the Andean range.
- **[CalTopo / SARtopo](https://caltopo.com/)** - High-level backcountry mapping suite featuring customizable contour intervals, slope-angle shading (crucial for avalanche avoidance), and satellite fire layers.
- **[Camptocamp (C2C)](https://www.camptocamp.org/)** - Collaborative alpine and ski-mountaineering guidebook platform with a growing collection of Andean itineraries and real-time trip reports.
- **[Instituto Geográfico Militar de Chile (IGM)](https://www.igm.cl/)** - Official Chilean military geographic institute providing standard 1:50,000 and 1:250,000 topographic charts.
- **[Instituto Geográfico Nacional de Argentina (IGN)](https://www.ign.gob.ar/)** - Official national cartographic agency of Argentina providing topographic layers and geodetic reference models.
- **[OpenAndroMaps (South America)](https://www.openandromaps.org/)** - Free high-density vector topo maps covering Chile and Argentina, compatible with offline GPS apps like OruxMaps and Locus Map using the Elevate theme.
- **[OpenTopoMap](https://opentopomap.org/)** - OpenStreetMap-based rendered topographic contour maps with hillshading for route navigation.
- **[Strava Global Heatmap](https://www.strava.com/heatmap)** - Visual aggregate of athlete GPS recordings useful for discerning actual footpaths across trackless Andean scree and ridgelines.
- **[Wikiexplora Andes](https://www.wikiexplora.com/)** - Collaborative non-profit platform providing topographical route descriptions, access roads, and free downloadable GPX tracks throughout Chile and the Central Andes.

## Mobile Navigation & Field Tools

- **[Gaia GPS](https://www.gaiagps.com/)** - Backcountry navigation application offering layered topo maps, satellite downloads, and offline breadcrumb tracking.
- **[Organic Maps](https://organicmaps.app/)** - Lightweight, battery-efficient, and privacy-focused offline mapping tool with altitude profiles and waypoint management.
- **[OsmAnd](https://osmand.net/)** - Powerful offline navigation app utilizing OpenStreetMap data, hillshading plugins, offline contour lines, and GPX track recording.
- **[PeakFinder](https://www.peakfinder.org/)** - Generates a 360° panoramic horizon view from any coordinate on Earth completely offline, naming every peak and tracking solar/lunar trajectories.
- **[PeakVisor](https://peakvisor.com/)** - Augmented reality mobile app identifying mountain peaks in 3D through phone camera optics using offline elevation models.
- **[PhotoPills](https://www.photopills.com/)** - Field planning app for high-altitude astrophotography, twilight calculation, Milky Way galactic core alignment, and solar eclipse tracking in dark-sky locations.

## Permits, National Parks & Border Formalities

- **[ALMA Observatory Safety Protocols](https://www.almaobservatory.org/)** - High-altitude safety procedures and access rules for the Chajnantor plateau (affecting ascents near Cerro Toco and Sairecabur).
- **[CONAF (Chilean National Forest Corporation)](https://www.conaf.cl/)** - Official management authority for National Parks and Nature Reserves (Los Flamencos National Reserve, Llullaillaco National Park, Nevado Tres Cruces).
- **[DIFROL Chile (Border & Frontier Directorate)](https://difrol.gob.cl/)** - Mandatory online permit authority for foreign expeditions planning ascents within Chile's border and frontier security zones.
- **[Parque Provincial Aconcagua Permit Portal](https://www.mendoza.gov.ar/ambiente/areas-protegidas/aconcagua/)** - Official Government of Mendoza portal for mandatory trekking and summit climbing permits, medical checks, and ranger station registration.
- **[SERNANP Peru](https://www.gob.pe/sernanp)** - National service of protected natural areas in Peru, regulating climbing permits and guide requirements in Huascarán National Park and Cordillera Blanca.
- **[SERNAP Bolivia](https://sernap.gob.bo/)** - Protected areas authority in Bolivia, overseeing permits for Nevado Sajama National Park and Eduardo Avaroa Andean Fauna National Reserve.

## Mountain Rescue & Satellite Communications

- **[Cuerpo de Socorro Andino (CSA Chile)](https://www.socorroandinochile.cl/)** - Volunteer mountain rescue and high-altitude search operation agency operating throughout Chile.
- **[Garmin inReach / Iridium Satellite Network](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/765374)** - Critical standard for two-way SOS communication and tracking in the high Atacama desert where cellular service is non-existent.
- **[Garmin Response (formerly GEOS SAR)](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/garmin-response/)** - 24/7 dedicated global monitoring center coordinating search and rescue responses with local authorities and military aviation assets.
- **[GOPE Carabineros de Chile](https://www.carabineros.cl/)** - Special Police Operations Group responsible for wilderness rescue, missing person operations, and emergency field extractions.
- **[Patrulla de Rescate de Alta Montaña de Mendoza (UPRAM)](https://www.policia.mendoza.gov.ar/)** - Specialist high-altitude police rescue squad operating permanently in Mount Aconcagua and the Central Argentine Andes.
- **[Wilderness VHF Emergency Radio Protocol](https://www.theuiaa.org/)** - Standard mountain radio emergency channel procedures; in Chile, VHF 146.520 MHz and local Socorro Andino repeater channels are monitored during expedition seasons.
- **[ZOLEO Satellite Communicator](https://www.zoleo.com/)** - Seamless cellular/satellite messaging device operating over the Iridium satellite network with dedicated SOS dispatch.

## Mountain Guide Associations & Climbing Federations

- **[AAGM Argentina (Asociación Argentina de Guías de Montaña)](https://aagm.com.ar/)** - The national mountaineering and ski guide association of Argentina, member of the IFMGA.
- **[AGMP Peru (Asociación de Guías de Montaña del Perú)](https://www.agmp.pe/)** - Peruvian mountain guides association operating in the Cordillera Blanca and Huayhuash, affiliated with the IFMGA.
- **[ANGM Chile (Asociación Nacional de Guías de Montaña)](https://angm.cl/)** - Official member association of the IFMGA in Chile representing certified mountain and trekking guides.
- **[Club Alemán Andino (DAV Chile)](https://dav.cl/)** - The oldest mountaineering club in Chile (founded in 1924), promoting mountain safety, route conservation, and mountaineering culture.
- **[Club Andino Bariloche (CAB Argentina)](https://www.clubandino.org/)** - Founded in 1931 in Northern Patagonia, managing an extensive mountain refuge network and volunteer mountain rescue commissions.
- **[FEACH (Federación de Andinismo de Chile)](https://feach.cl/)** - National governing federation for sport climbing, mountaineering, and mountain expeditions in Chile.
- **[FASA (Federación Argentina de Ski y Andinismo)](https://fasa.org.ar/)** - National sports federation for ski-mountaineering and mountaineering in Argentina.
- **[IFMGA / UIAGM (International Federation of Mountain Guides Associations)](https://ifmga.info/)** - The international governing body establishing global certification standards for high-mountain guides.
- **[UIAA (International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation)](https://www.theuiaa.org/)** - Global federation representing climbers and mountaineers, setting international climbing safety and equipment standards.

## Classic Literature & Historic Expedition Archives

- **[American Alpine Journal (AAJ) Digital Archives](https://publications.americanalpineclub.org/)** - Searchable historical repository of expedition records, first ascents, and new routes across the Andes documented since 1929 by the American Alpine Club.
- **[PataClimb Patagonia Route Database](https://www.pataclimb.com/)** - Rolando Garibotti's definitive online guidebook to the Chaltén Massif (Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre), detailing every route, history, and gear requirement.
- **[The Andes: A Guide for Climbers and Skiers by John Biggar](https://www.andes.org.uk/)** - The definitive English-language reference book and website cataloging all 100+ summits over 6,000 meters in the Andes with route topos and approach logistics.
- **[The Andes: Complete History of Mountaineering in High South America](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0996248906)** - Evelio Echevarría's magnum opus chronicling centuries of indigenous high-altitude sanctuaries and modern alpine ascents.
- **[The Ice Maiden: Mountain Gods & Inca Sanctuaries](https://www.johanreinhard.org/)** - Anthropologist Johan Reinhard's seminal account of discovering the preserved Llullaillaco mummies and Ampato frozen sacrifices atop Andean volcanic summits.

## Conservation, Leave No Trace & High-Altitude Ethics

- **[Acceso PanAm](https://accesopanam.org/)** - Pan-American coalition dedicated to preserving public access, promoting land conservation, and advocating for climbing areas across Latin America.
- **[High-Altitude Bofedales & Peatland Preservation](https://ramsar.org/)** - Conservation guidelines for fragile Andean peat bogs (*bofedales*) that serve as critical water reservoirs and habitats for vicuñas, flamingos, and endemic wildlife.
- **[Human Waste Pack-Out Protocols (WAG Bags & Cleanwaste)](https://cleanwaste.com/)** - Essential waste management methods for high camps on rock, scree, and ice where sub-zero arid conditions prevent biological decomposition.
- **[Leave No Trace (LNT) in Cryosphere & Arid Environments](https://lnt.org/)** - Wilderness ethics adapted for hyper-arid Puna deserts and freezing glacial environments where organic waste, toilet paper, and trash never biodegrade.
- **[Respecting Indigenous Sacred Summits (Apus)](https://www.unesco.org/)** - Ethical guidelines when visiting pre-Columbian ceremonial platforms, respecting spiritual traditions, leaving Incan archaeological stone enclosures (*pircas*) intact, and reporting new findings to national heritage institutions.
